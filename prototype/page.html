About us.

A family-based ministry devoted to teaching, exalting, loving, and living out the grace of Christ in Italy.

01 / Our story

Naples, Britain, Tuscany.

We are a family-based ministry. Manuela and Vincenzo—usually Vince—are mum and dad; Cristina, Salvo, and Emma complete the Russo family.

Manuela and Vince are originally from Naples. After nearly fifteen years in the United Kingdom, a long-held calling brought the family back to Italy, where they now live and serve in Tuscany.

Their backgrounds in languages, technology, biblical exegesis, and theology meet in a ministry centred on careful teaching, translation, hospitality, and church life.

Rooted in two cultures

Italy is home. The UK remains a deeply loved part of the family’s story.

03 / What we do

Grace made practical.

Our work includes Italian-language writing through Per Grazia, translating and publishing books, preaching in Italian and English, conferences, deeper teaching, and intimate fellowship at home.

Each expression serves the same purpose: the edification of the Church through the exaltation of Christ and the whole counsel of Scripture.
